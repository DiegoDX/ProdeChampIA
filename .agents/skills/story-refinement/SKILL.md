---
name: story-refinement
description: Refine ProdeChamp user stories and identify acceptance-criteria gaps when planning approved product work.
---

Read repository `AGENTS.md`, relevant `docs/product/` material and the target story in `docs/user-stories/`.

Separate approved requirements from assumptions and open questions. Identify observable acceptance criteria, business ambiguities, dependencies and scope boundaries. Ask for clarification when a business rule affects behavior. Do not invent scoring or competition rules.

Produce a proposed story refinement and a verification outline. Preserve existing acceptance criteria unless the user authorizes a change. This skill does not authorize implementation or expansion of the requested story.
